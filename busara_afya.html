Busara Afya (meaning "Health Wisdom") is a community health education initiative founded in Tanzania, dedicated to closing the gap between evidence-based health knowledge and everyday practice in communities, with a particular focus on adolescents and youth.

We believe that every young person deserves access to accurate, reliable, and culturally relevant health information — information that empowers them to take control of their well-being and advocate for their communities.

Through digital content, community outreach, and strategic partnerships with health professionals, Busara Afya is building a generation of informed, health-literate citizens.

Our Purpose

Vision & Mission

Vision

A generation of young people empowered with evidence-based health knowledge to make informed decisions, break down health myths, and build healthier communities for themselves and future generations.

Mission

To educate and empower adolescents and youth with evidence-based health information through engaging digital content that debunks myths, promotes preventive health practices, and fosters critical thinking about community health — grounded in rigorous research and delivered in accessible, relatable ways.

What We Stand For

Core Values

Every piece of content we create, every campaign we run, and every partnership we forge is guided by these foundational principles.

01

Evidence-Based

All content is rooted in peer-reviewed research and verified health science. We never publish without credible sources.

02

Youth-Centered

Content is designed by and for young people. We speak their language, address their realities, and honor their intelligence.

03

Accessible

Complex health topics made understandable for everyone — regardless of education level or background. Health literacy for all.

04

Empowering

Building health literacy and critical thinking so communities can advocate for themselves and reject misinformation.

05

Inclusive

Addressing diverse community health needs — across gender, geography, and socioeconomic background — with equity at our core.

06

Transparent

Clear sourcing, honest acknowledgment of limitations, and immediate corrections when needed. Trust is everything.

What We Do

Our Weekly Programs

A structured weekly cadence of health education content designed to inform, engage, and empower communities.

Myth-Busting Thursdays

Every Thursday

Debunking common health myths using research evidence. From traditional misconceptions to modern misinformation — we expose the facts with science.

Biggie's Wellness Wednesday

Every Wednesday

Preventive health practices, nutrition, exercise, and mental health tips — all grounded in science. Practical wellness guidance for everyday life.

Tafiti Fridays

Every Friday

Quick health statistics and infographics covering diseases, vaccines, reproductive health, and more. Research insights in a digestible, shareable format.

Nondo Mondays

Every Monday

Deep-dive education on a specific community health topic each week — from reproductive health to chronic disease management. Structured, in-depth learning for all.

Q&A Sessions

Interactive & Live

Open Q&A where community members submit health questions and receive evidence-based answers from Dr. Mwakitinya and guest health professionals.

Campaign Series

Monthly Themes

Multi-week themed campaigns aligned with global health calendar events — Mental Health Month, World Malaria Day, and more — for sustained community impact.

Meet the Founder

Dr. Ezekiel Mwakitinya

Daktari Mkazi wa Afya ya Jamii · Community Medicine Physician

Dr. Ezekiel Mwakitinya is a dedicated Community Medicine Physician based in Tanzania whose passion for health equity led him to found Busara Afya. With years of clinical experience and a deep understanding of the health challenges facing Tanzanian communities, he recognized a critical gap: life-saving health information was not reaching the people who needed it most.

Through his work at the Royal Clinic and beyond, Dr. Mwakitinya has witnessed firsthand how health myths and misinformation cost lives — and how simple, accurate health education can transform communities.

"Niwaombe ndugu wataalamu wa Afya kupaza sauti na kukemea matatizo hayo kwani yanarudisha nyuma jitihada ambazo zimeshafanywa na mfumo mzima wa afya nchini kuwafikia wananchi walipo."

— Dr. Ezekiel Mwakitinya

His vision extends beyond social media — he is building a comprehensive health education ecosystem that includes a podcast, long-form video content, student-centered education, telemedicine through Royal Clinic, and NGO partnerships to scale impact across East Africa.
